Circuit Simulator

Place parts on the breadboard. When there's a full loop from the battery's + end, through a bulb, and back to its − end, the bulb lights up.

Parts

How it works
  • Pick a part, then tap two holes. The part's two legs go in those holes.
  • For the battery, the first hole is the + end and the second is the − end.
  • Holes in the same shaded column strip are joined inside the board, so parts in the same strip are connected.
  • Tap a switch to open or close it.
  • Pick the Eraser and tap a part to remove it.
